- 🏆 **Achievements.** Earn 20 achievements for island milestones, such as never getting stranded, sweeping the chimney before it's a fire risk, winning at bingo, making a close friend, or surviving Ferry Hell. They're saved on your device and carry over to every new game and save slot. See them in ⚙️ Settings → 🏆 Achievements, where locked ones tell you how to earn them. The end screen lists any you earned that game.
- 💾 **Three save slots.** Keep up to three games going at once. On the title screen, tap 💾 Save slots to continue, start a new game in, or delete each slot. While you play, ⚙️ Settings → 💾 Save slot lets you switch slots, and your current week is saved first. Your existing save is now slot 1, so nothing is lost.
- Continue, Start a new week, Exit and transfer codes all use your current slot.
