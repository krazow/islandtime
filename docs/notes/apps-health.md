# Health apps

- 🩺 **Telehealth** (new, in the App Store). Video visits with a real doctor, once a day: 20 minutes, a $25 copay and a chunk of battery. It needs real signal, so it works on the mainland or on the Riv’s Wi-Fi. On the island’s one bar the video freezes on your own face.
  - 💊 **Renew your prescription** (Wednesday to Saturday, +$15 for the meds). It comes by mail and lands in your box the next afternoon around 1 PM. No pharmacy run, but it costs more than picking it up. Ask on Saturday and it arrives Sunday, after you’ve already run out that night.
  - 😮‍💨 **Stressed out?** A breathing exercise “for the ferry line.” Stress −6.
  - 🤒 **Run down?** Fluids, sleep, a vegetable. Health +3.
  - 🤕 **Your ankle:** a quick look on camera and “go to Urgent Care.” No charge. Telehealth doesn’t replace your Thursday checkup either.
- 📣 **Island Group** now shows your island reputation at the top, what it unlocks, and the last three replies to your posts.
- 🧹 **Retired apps.** Ferry Alerts, Job Hunt, Flashlight and Notes are gone from the App Store. Newer apps replace them. If you had any of them installed, the slots are free again, and you get a note about it once when you load your game.
