TITLE
Island Time 1.11: What's New (island help, Next up, Veterans Day and achievements)

DESCRIPTION
Island Time 1.11 is out. Island Time is a free browser game about a week, or a whole month, of living on Anderson Island, Washington, where one small county ferry connects you to your job, the store, gas and the doctor.

What's new in 1.11:
🧭 Next up: a pinned card tells you the one thing that matters now, like which boat gets you to work on time. Tap it to open the ferry trip planner.
🏠 Your house: daily chores pile up if you skip them. Spoiled fridges, damp windows and woodstove ash all need you.
🌲 Outside and property: gutters, roof moss, potholes and the well. Contractors ride the ferry, so they're late when it's down.
🧰 Gus, the island handyman: he fixes the three worst jobs. Pay in cash, gas or groceries. He runs on island time.
📣 The community group: post for help, help your neighbors first, and more people show up for you. Make new friends by pitching in.
🇺🇸 Island events all month: Veterans Day at the Legion, a holiday craft bazaar, lake cleanup day, a Fire District #27 drill, and story arcs with your friends.
🏆 16 achievements that stay with you across every game.
💾 Three save slots: keep up to three islanders going on the same device.
