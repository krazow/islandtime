TITLE
Island Time 1.12: Your Phone (texts, calls and an App Store on Anderson Island)

DESCRIPTION
Island Time 1.12 puts a phone in your pocket. Island Time is a free browser game about a week, or a whole month, of living on Anderson Island, Washington, where one small county ferry connects you to your job, the store, gas and the doctor.

What's new in 1.12:
📱 Your phone: tap the phone in the top bar. Its battery is the one the game already tracks, so a dead phone means no texts or calls.
💬 Messages: text everyone you've met on the island. Quick replies, unread dots, and friends who text you first. Replies take island time.
🚗 Ask Sam for a ride: text or call him from home before 10:30 AM. If he says yes, "Ride with Sam" shows up at the ferry landing. (Sam no longer turns up on his own.)
🩴 Pajama Fred hears things: once he's a good friend, his buddy on the deck crew warns you before the next ferry breakdown.
📞 Phone calls: catch up with friends to lower your stress and grow the friendship. They call you too, and if nobody picks up, leave a voicemail.
🛍️ App Store with 5 slots: Island Group, Ferry Alerts, Job Hunt, Flashlight and Notes. With one bar of signal on the island, an install takes six minutes.
⛴️ Ferry Alerts: next sailings, rider alerts and fog delays in your pocket.
