Island Time

Survive a week on Anderson Island, Washington, where one small county ferry connects you to your job, the store, gas and the doctor.

⛴️ Play now
Works offline after the first visit. Open it once with internet, then add it to your home screen. It runs like an app, with no app store and no account. Your saved games stay on your phone.

Scan with your phone camera

krazow.github.io/islandtime

📱 iPhone or iPad

  1. Open krazow.github.io/islandtime in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Island Time is now on your home screen.

Safari works best. On recent iOS versions, Chrome and Edge also offer “Add to Home Screen” in their Share menu.

🤖 Android

  1. Open krazow.github.io/islandtime in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones it says Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install. Open it from your home screen or app drawer.

You can also tap Install as an app on the game's title screen.

Other ways to play

  • 🪟 Windows installer: download IslandTime-Setup.exe. Installs per-user, no admin needed.
  • 📴 Single-file offline version: one HTML file with everything inside. Save it and double-click it, with no internet or server needed.
  • 🌐 Any browser on any computer: krazow.github.io/islandtime
